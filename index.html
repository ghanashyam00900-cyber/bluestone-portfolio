Cut notes

Why it works

Three pieces I study: what each one does, what I’d steal, and what I’d try next. Not affiliated with BlueStone, Mia by Tanishq or their agencies.

BlueStone · #ThreesToOne · 2024 · Manja · six short films for YouTube and Instagram

A festival told as a serial.

A Raksha Bandhan serial about the husband’s best friend, the “third wheel”, who becomes family.

Structure
Five light films build a habit; the sixth pays off with a rakhi and BlueStone earrings.
Insight
A bond jewellery ads usually skip: the friend who is always around. No couple, bride or heirloom.
Craft and medium
Feed-sized: the first three ran 20 seconds and drew views “in lakhs”. ThePrint called it witty and not over-sentimental.
What I’d steal
Serialise the festival, so people turn up before the day.
What I’d try next
Plant the piece early in the series, so the finale pays off something viewers have already seen.

BlueStone · Big Gold Upgrade · 2023 · Manja

An offer told as a story.

An old-gold exchange (18K valued as 22K, 22K as 24K), launched with a film about treasured jewellery found in lockers and trunks. Still on BlueStone’s site in Nov 2026.

Structure
The hero is the forgotten jewellery, and the discount follows from it.
Insight
Families keep gold they never wear. BlueStone’s 2026 line, “lockers to the wardrobe”, was already here in 2023.
Craft and medium
It ran across print, cinema, OOH, digital, retail, audio and TV. A one-line mechanic travels.
What I’d steal
A value mechanic with an emotion inside it.
What I’d try next
Give the new design as much screen time as the old gold, since BlueStone calls its designs the heroes.

Mia by Tanishq · “Precious, every day” · 2025

The line became the format.

A film with Aneet Padda (Sep 2025), then calendar billboards in Mumbai and Bengaluru where each date lights up a different piece (Nov 2025).

Structure
“Every day” becomes a calendar, which renews itself.
Insight
Ordinary days rarely feel precious, and Mia calls self-purchase one of its fastest-growing segments.
Craft and medium
Built for OOH: it borrows a daily habit, the same logic as Heineken’s 2026 Cannes Social & Creator Grand Prix.
What I’d steal
Give each medium its own version of the idea.
What I’d try next
A phone calendar: one piece per date, styled for that day.

Every new store city

The Function Counter, in that city. A local creator runs a Bas Aise Hi episode in the city’s language in launch week (all 7 cities BlueStone entered in Q2 FY27 were Tier 2 or 3). Creator Reel · Store Stories · a 4:5 card that works on WhatsApp.

  1. The design is the hero.A real piece, at true size, inside the first 2 seconds.
  2. No fake sparkle.No star filters, no added glints, no skin lightening.
  3. Earn a save, a share or a laugh.If a post does none of the three, it doesn’t ship.
  4. What I’d measure.Saves and shares per 1,000 reach, 3-second hold, and taps to Try at Home or the store locator.
